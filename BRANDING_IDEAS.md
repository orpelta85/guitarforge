## Part 2: Logo Directions

### Direction 1: GuitarForge (Current Name)

**Concept:** A guitar headstock morphing into an anvil, with a subtle flame or spark element. The "G" in GuitarForge could be stylized as a tuning peg or the curve of a guitar body.

- **Color Palette:** Deep black (#0a0a0a) background, amber/molten metal (#f59e0b) as primary, with ember gradients (amber to deep orange #ea580c to red #dc2626)
- **Typography:** Bold geometric sans-serif. Something like "Instrument Serif" for the wordmark with a custom-cut feel -- angular, sharp edges. Weight 700+.
- **Icon:** Minimalist anvil silhouette with a guitar neck rising from it. A single spark/ember dot above. Works at 16x16 favicon size as just the anvil-neck shape.
- **Mood:** Craftsmanship, heat, transformation. Like a blacksmith's mark.

### Direction 2: RiffForge

**Concept:** Two interlocking "R" and "F" letters forming a monogram that resembles a guitar pick. The negative space between the letters creates a lightning bolt or sound wave shape.

- **Color Palette:** Matte black base, electric amber (#f59e0b), with a secondary steel gray (#71717a) for depth
- **Typography:** Custom condensed sans-serif, slightly italicized to suggest motion/speed. Think of racing typography -- forward-leaning energy.
- **Icon:** The RF monogram works standalone. Inside a rounded-square app icon, it sits on a dark background with a subtle radial gradient suggesting heat.
- **Mood:** Speed, precision, modern. A tool for serious players.

### Direction 3: Shredd

**Concept:** The word "SHREDD" in a custom typeface where the letters appear to be sliced/shredded -- with horizontal cut lines through them, like claw marks or pick scratches across metal. The double-D creates a visual rhythm.

- **Color Palette:** Pure black and white as primary (maximum contrast), with amber used only as an accent/highlight on the slash marks
- **Typography:** Custom display face built specifically for the brand. Heavy weight, condensed, with the signature "slash" treatment. Body text in a clean grotesque (Inter, Helvetica Neue).
- **Icon:** A single stylized "S" with the slash treatment works as a favicon/app icon. Alternatively, two parallel diagonal slashes (like pick scratches).
- **Mood:** Aggressive, bold, unapologetic. This is not a gentle learning app -- this is a training ground.

### Direction 4: FretLab

**Concept:** A clean, modern mark combining a fret/grid pattern with a laboratory flask or beaker silhouette. The fret lines double as measurement markings on the flask. Science meets music.

- **Color Palette:** Dark charcoal (#171717) base, amber (#f59e0b) primary, with a cool blue-gray (#94a3b8) secondary for the "lab" feel. The contrast between warm amber and cool gray creates visual tension.
- **Typography:** Monospaced or semi-monospaced for the wordmark (like JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono) -- reinforces the technical/analytical identity. Clean and precise.
- **Icon:** A minimalist flask/beaker outline with fret-line markings inside. At small sizes, simplifies to a diamond shape (flask from the side) with horizontal lines.
- **Mood:** Precision, data-driven, modern. For the player who tracks everything and optimizes their practice like an engineer.

### Direction 5: IronFret

**Concept:** Raw, industrial aesthetic. The logo looks stamped or pressed into metal -- like a maker's mark on a steel beam. Texture is key: brushed metal, rivets, industrial patina.

- **Color Palette:** Gunmetal (#374151), rust orange (#c2410c), aged brass (#b45309), on near-black (#0a0a0a). The palette feels like it belongs in a foundry or machine shop.
- **Typography:** Slab serif with heavy weight (like Rockwell, Clarendon, or a custom industrial slab). All caps. Letter-spacing slightly wide. Feels stamped.
- **Icon:** A guitar fret wire bent into an "I" shape, or a single fret segment with an industrial rivet at each end. Simple, geometric, brutal.
- **Mood:** Heavy, uncompromising, built to last. This app is a tool, not a toy. For players who treat their instrument like a weapon.

GuitarForge is built for serious rock and metal players -- with 8,600+ songs, Guitar Pro tab playback, AI-powered coaching, a built-in recording studio with Suno AI backing tracks, and a skill tree that turns practice into measurable progression.
